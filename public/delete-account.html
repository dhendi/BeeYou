BeeYou Account & Data Deletion

Self-Service & Web Request Portal

Permanent Data Erasure Warning Deleting your BeeYou family account is permanent and irreversible. It immediately purges your family login credentials, all uploaded custom speech photos and voice recordings, cloud Realtime Database channels, backend server pairing sessions, and wipes all local cached data from this device.
Important: App Store & Google Play Subscription Advisory Deleting your BeeYou account does not automatically cancel an active Apple App Store or Google Play Store subscription. Because payments and renewals are managed directly by Apple or Google under your personal store account, you must cancel your subscription directly through your device settings to prevent future renewals:
Manage Apple App Store Subscription → Manage Google Play Subscription →

How to Delete Your Account

Method 1: In-App Instant Deletion (Recommended)

If you have the BeeYou app installed or opened in your browser, you can initiate immediate self-service account deletion:

Launch In-App Account Deletion

Or inside the app: Navigate to Caregiver Hub > Link Device > Delete Family Account, enter your account password, and confirm deletion.

Method 2: Email Deletion Request

If you no longer have access to the app or device, you may request manual deletion by emailing our Data Protection Officer:

  • Email: privacy@beeyou.app
  • Include: Your registered parent/caregiver account email address and family sync code.
  • Processing Time: Completed within 48 business hours with confirmation email.

Data Types Deleted vs. Retained

  • Parent Account Email & Password Hash: Permanently deleted immediately.
  • Child Profile, Mood Logs & Visual Schedules: Permanently purged from cloud databases.
  • Uploaded AAC Photos & Voice Recordings: Permanently deleted from storage buckets.
  • Linked Caregiver & Child Devices: Access revoked instantly; real-time sessions disconnected.
  • Retained Data: None. Zero personal data is retained following completion of account deletion.
