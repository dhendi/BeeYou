Legal & Child Safety Transparency

Privacy Policy & COPPA Disclosure

Effective Date: December 1, 2026 | Version 1.0

Our Core Privacy Commitment BeeYou is designed for neurodivergent children, teens, and families. We adhere strictly to Apple App Store Kids Category guidelines, Google Play Families Policy, the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), and GDPR-K. We have zero advertisements, zero third-party tracking SDKs, and never sell personal information.

1. Children's Online Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

BeeYou does not knowingly collect personal identification information directly from children under the age of 13 without verified parental consent. Family accounts are registered and managed exclusively by parents, legal guardians, or educational professionals (18+). Communication tiles, mood logs, sensory regulation logs, and visual routines are treated as confidential family data.

2. Zero Advertising & Zero Tracking SDKs

We believe assistive neurodivergent software should be a calm, distraction-free sanctuary. BeeYou contains:

  • No third-party commercial advertisements, banners, or interstitials.
  • No cross-app tracking pixels, advertising identifiers (IDFA/GAID), or behavioral profiling cookies.
  • No monetization or resale of sensitive personal, emotional, or medical logs.

3. Information We Collect and How It Is Used

We collect only the minimum data necessary to deliver assistive communication and companion sync:

  • Parent Account Email: Used exclusively for secure sign-in, account authentication, and password/PIN resets.
  • 7-Character Sync Code: A cryptographically unguessable random code used to connect a child tablet with a caregiver phone without exposing personal identities.
  • Custom AAC Symbols & Voice Prompts: Custom photos and voice recordings chosen by families are stored locally on-device and synchronized across your private, encrypted family channel.

4. Sensitive Health, Prescription & Menstrual Cycle Data (Apple Guideline 5.1.3)

BeeYou includes specialized tools to support neurodivergent individuals and families with executive function, sensory awareness, and daily health routines. We treat this data with the highest level of privacy and sensitivity:

  • Prescription & Medication Reminders: Names of medications, custom dosages, scheduled reminder times, administration notes (such as “take with food”), and dosage confirmation logs are strictly private family data.
  • Menstrual Cycle & Period Tracking: Cycle dates, flow intensity, physical and emotional symptoms, and body awareness logs are maintained solely for personal sensory pacing and routine preparation.
  • Strict Anti-Monetization & Non-Disclosure Guarantee: In accordance with Apple App Store Guideline 5.1.3 and global health data protections, your health, medication, and cycle records are NEVER sold, NEVER rented, NEVER shared with third-party advertisers, data brokers, or health insurance companies, and are NEVER used for behavioral targeting or commercial profiling.
  • Discreet Mode for Sensory & Social Privacy: A built-in discreet toggle allows users to disguise cycle tracking as “Wellness Rhythm” with neutral icons, preventing unintentional disclosure on shared screens in classrooms or public settings.

5. Security, Encryption & On-Device Sanitization

All network traffic is encrypted using TLS 1.3 (HTTPS & Secure WebSockets). Caregiver PINs are salt-hashed using SHA-256 before being stored in local device memory. For shared school loaner tablets, our 1-tap “Erase & Reset Device” tool purges all local medication records, routines, cycle logs, and journal entries.

6. Apple Guideline 5.1.1(v) & Google Play In-App Account Deletion

Caregivers have the right to permanently delete their account and all associated data at any time directly within the app or online. Tapping “Delete Family Account” in the Caregiver Link Tab removes your Firebase Auth record, purges your cloud database channel, and wipes local device storage immediately. Users can also request deletion online at beeyou.app/?action=delete-account.

7. Third-Party Services Audit & Data Safeguards

BeeYou minimizes third-party dependencies:

  • Authentication & Cloud Sync: Google Firebase Authentication & Realtime Database are used strictly for private family state sync. Firebase Analytics and Advertising features are disabled.
  • Speech Synthesis (TTS): Text-to-speech runs 100% on-device using the operating system's native speech synthesis engine. Speech text is never sent to external AI servers.
  • Translation: Multi-language translation runs locally using pre-bundled JSON dictionaries.
  • In-App Purchases: Managed directly through standard Apple StoreKit and Google Play Billing APIs.

8. Contact Our Privacy Officer

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, your family's data, or wish to exercise data rights, contact us directly at privacy@beeyou.app.
